A year after the [first visit](/en/blog/oe1w-traisnerhuette/) I was back at the **Traisnerhütte** on the Muckenkogel above Lilienfeld, **1,313 metres**, locator **JN77TX**. On the first weekend of September the **IARU Region 1 VHF contest** runs there on 144 MHz: from **Saturday, 5 September, 16:00 to Sunday, 6 September, 16:00 local time** — twenty-four hours without a break.

This time I wasn't just there to watch. For a few hours I got to help out myself, at the rig and beside it.

![Kenwood TS-890 with the Kuhne TR 144 Pro transverter and the Alinco power supply above it, the microHAM micro KEYER II on the left, the note reading OE1W JN77TX on the right](../../../assets/photos/oe1w-ts890-transverter.jpg)

## The weather, the band

Glorious weather, a cloudless sky over the whole weekend. The band was the same: conditions you wish for in a contest.

The longest QSO of the weekend went **1,005 kilometres** — on two metres, with nothing but the antenna and whatever the troposphere was giving that day.

![Two masts on the meadow against a deep blue sky, someone working at the right-hand mast](../../../assets/photos/oe1w-antennen-aufbau.jpg)

## Three at the rig

There were three of us operating: **Rainer, OE3REC**, **OE5LHM** and me, **OE5SOS**. Whoever wasn't on the microphone was logging, turning the antennas or simply listening.

![Two operators with headsets at the desks, the laptop with the contest log in the foreground](../../../assets/photos/oe1w-im-shack.jpg)

## When the sun sits low

A twenty-four-hour contest has two twilights, and both are among the finest hours up there. The yagis stand as lines against the sky, and when an aircraft passes it writes its own line across them.

![Two stacked yagis on the mast against the evening sky, a contrail running right across the frame, mountain ridges below](../../../assets/photos/oe1w-yagis-abendhimmel.jpg)

## Thanks

Many thanks to **Rainer, OE3REC**, for the invitation, and to everyone who made the weekend possible — for letting me take part this time instead of just watching.
