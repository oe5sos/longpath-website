The **Puig de Santa Magdalena** stands alone in the plain above **Inca**, in the middle of Mallorca. **305 metres**, reference **EA6/MA-060**, two points, locator **JM19lr**. A road leads to the top; from the car park it is a few hundred metres on foot. Not a mountain, but a summit — and up there you stand in the open, looking across the whole island to the coast.

![View from the Puig de Santa Magdalena over pine forest and an industrial estate down into the plain, the coast and the deep blue sea beyond](../../../assets/photos/santa-magdalena-blick.jpg)

## The rig

Not the KX2 this time but the **IC-705** — as **EA6/OE5SOS/P**. In the afternoon it was running on **14.276 MHz** USB.

![The Icom IC-705 lying on its bag, the display showing 14.276.005 in USB, the red and black power lead beside it](../../../assets/photos/santa-magdalena-ic705.jpg)

## The contacts

**Twelve QSOs**, all SSB: eleven on 20 metres, one on 40. From the island the whole of Europe fans out to the north-east — England, the Netherlands, Poland, Czechia, Slovenia, Moldova, Greece; plus Navarre and, the only one on 40 metres, Andalusia.

![Map from the SOTA database: dashed yellow lines run from Mallorca to Leeds, Groningen, Kraków, Brno, Slovenia, Moldova, Patras and Pamplona, an orange one to Andalusia](../../../assets/karten/santa-magdalena-karte.png)
