Two summits, one day, 23 kilometres, 5:30 hours on the move. Weather
you'd order if you could: clear sight to the limestone walls in the
background, barely any wind, not a cloud in the way.

## The approach to the Schwalbenmauer

This is the part worth knowing beforehand.

**The route isn't signposted.** There's no waymarking to hang onto.
Anyone wanting to go up should already be comfortable with
trackless terrain — very doable for the practised, not for anyone
else.

**A navigation device is well worth having.** Not for convenience,
but because there's no second point of orientation.

**There's no water.** No spring, no stream, nothing along the way.
Whatever you need you carry up from below — and in that weather you
need more than you think.

## The Kasberg

Ninety metres higher and considerably friendlier to reach. From the
summit the whole range lies open, and you can see just how far
you've come.
