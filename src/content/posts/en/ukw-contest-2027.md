Twice now I have been at the **IARU Region 1 VHF contest** on the **Traisnerhütte**, 1,313 metres, JN77TX. [2025](/en/blog/oe1w-traisnerhuette/) as a guest, watching a station work its way to sixth place out of 193. [2026](/en/blog/oe1w-contest-2026/) with a few hours at the rig myself, in conditions that reached 1,005 kilometres.

The contest runs every year on the first weekend of September, from Saturday 16:00 to Sunday 16:00. Next year that is **4 and 5 September 2027**.

I'm coming. No longer to watch — as part of the **OE1W** team.

![Two stacked Yagis on the mast against the evening sky, a contrail running across the picture, the mountain ranges below](../../../assets/photos/oe1w-yagis-abendhimmel.jpg)

A year to go.
